Why use PCA with under 10 features on American Electric Power data?

Hourly lag features (lag_1 and lag_24) have extreme autocorrelation (Pearson correlation r > 0.88). PCA eliminates multicollinearity by compressing the continuous load dynamics into 2 uncorrelated principal components, accelerating search and spatial clustering while preserving over 90% of total variance.
