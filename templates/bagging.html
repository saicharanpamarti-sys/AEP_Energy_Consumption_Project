Variance Reduction for AEP Peak Load Surges Grid Engineering

Why Random Forest excels for American Electric Power hourly dispatch over individual decision trees:

  • Subspace Randomization: Selecting random feature subsets at each node decorrelates individual trees, dramatically shrinking variance on correlated lag variables.
  • Large Forest Convergence: As more bootstrap trees are aggregated, the variance of the average prediction shrinks toward zero without inflating bias, preventing overfitting.
  • Outlier Damping: Extreme temperature load spikes during winter freezes and summer heatwaves are smoothed across the forest, delivering a test RMSE of 242.41 MW.
