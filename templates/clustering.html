4. Model-Based: Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) Soft Transitions

Unlike hard clustering methods that force binary cluster assignments, Gaussian Mixture Models represent the continuous energy demand curve as a soft probabilistic mixture of Gaussian components, capturing gradual weather transitions between seasons.

BIC & AIC Model Selection Curve (K=1 to 8)

Parsimony Minimum: The Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC) penalizes model complexity and reaches its global minimum at K = 3 components, confirming that adding a 4th component overparameterizes the seasonal load distribution.

Soft Component Probabilities Across Calendar Months

Seasonal Transition Dynamics: Demonstrates continuous probabilistic shifts: Winter heating dominates Dec-Feb (P > 0.8), transitioning smoothly through mild shoulder conditions in April-May (P > 0.7), and peaking into summer cooling in July-August (P > 0.85).
