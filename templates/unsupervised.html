2. K-Means 24-Hour Load Profile Archetype Clustering Cluster Archetypes

Groups the 365 daily 24-hour demand curves into k = 3 distinct behavioral operational profiles:

Cluster 1: Winter Peakers

Dual-peaked curves with elevated morning (8 AM) and evening (7 PM) space heating loads during November through March.

Cluster 2: Summer Cooling Peakers

Sustained broad afternoon plateaus peaking between 2 PM and 6 PM driven by commercial air conditioning during June through August.

Cluster 3: Mild Shoulder Seasons

Lowest overall consumption levels during temperate spring (April-May) and autumn (October) months without heating/cooling stress.

Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Dimensionality Reduction Orthogonal Decomposition

Projects high-dimensional multi-lag temporal features (d = 24) into an orthogonal 2D/3D subspace maximizing variance:

PC 1 (Explained Variance)
~72.4%

~18.1%

Cumulative Variance (2 PCs)
~90.5%
Retains over 90% of structural variance in just 2 dimensions
Mathematical Formulation

Given sample covariance matrix Σ = (1/n) XT X, PCA computes orthogonal spectral eigenvectors vi:

Σ vi = λi vi,   where λ1 ≥ λ2 ≥ … ≥ λd

Enables 2D interactive projection of the 121,273 records, accelerating training of downstream classifiers while mitigating multicollinearity between adjacent hourly lags.
